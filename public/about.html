About the platform

Plumtree Connect

A local jobs and services board built for Plumtree town and the wards of Matabeleland South — Bulilima, Mangwe, Tshitshi, Matjinge, Empandeni and their neighbours.

What it does

Plumtree Connect brings two things together that usually live in separate places: paid work and the trades people need at home or at their business. Employers post vacancies; artisans and service businesses list what they do. Everyone searches the same board.

💼

Job board

Admin moderation

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, so the board stays honest and safe.

How it works

  1. Create an account as a job seeker, an employer or a service provider. Each role gets a dashboard suited to it.
  2. Employers and providers post listings. A job listing needs a title, description, category, area and contact details. A service listing needs a business name, description, services and a phone number.
  3. An administrator reviews it. Approved listings appear on the public board. Rejected listings carry a reason the owner can see and fix.
  4. Job seekers apply and track each application through submitted, reviewing, shortlisted, interview and hired. Employers move candidates through the same stages.
  5. Customers send service requests to providers, who reply with a quote and update the request status as work progresses.
  6. Customers rate providers from one to five stars. Ratings are shown publicly and can be moderated if abused.
